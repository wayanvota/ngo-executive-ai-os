# Contributing

This project is an experiment, not a maintained product.

Feedback is welcome, especially from nonprofit executives, fundraisers, board members, grant writers, and technologists who work with small or mid-sized NGOs. I may not be able to respond to every issue or act on every suggestion.

## Helpful Feedback

The most useful feedback is specific:

- what you tried
- where the instructions were unclear
- what kind of NGO context you were applying it to
- what felt risky, missing, or unrealistic
- what wording or workflow you would change

## Boundaries

Please do not post real donor, beneficiary, board, staff, financial, legal, or confidential organizational information in issues, discussions, pull requests, or examples.

If you share an example, sanitize it first.

## Pull Requests

Pull requests are welcome, but review may be slow or selective. I am most likely to consider changes that:

- improve clarity for nontechnical users
- strengthen privacy and permission boundaries
- add practical NGO fundraising or strategy routines
- improve source discipline
- reduce unsupported AI autonomy

I may close ideas that are useful but outside the scope of this experiment.
