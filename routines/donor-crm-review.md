# Donor CRM Review Routine

## Purpose

Review donor and funder relationships as an executive operating system, not as a generic contact list.

## Inputs To Review

1. `memory/fundraising-pipeline.md`
2. all relevant files in `memory/donors/`
3. `memory/commitments.md`
4. relevant meeting notes or source documents

## Review Logic

For each priority donor or prospect, assess:

- relationship status
- last meaningful contact
- next best action
- current ask, renewal, report, or cultivation step
- open commitments
- evidence needed
- likely objection
- risk of silence or delay
- owner

## Output

Produce a table with:

| Donor | Status | Next Action | Owner | Timing | Risk | Evidence Needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then write:

1. `Highest-leverage action this week`
2. `Relationships going stale`
3. `Asks that are not ready`
4. `Narratives that need evidence`
5. `Memory updates to make`

## Rules

- Do not rank by gift size alone.
- Prefer renewal risk and timing over speculative new prospects.
- Do not infer donor intent unless source-backed.
- Flag when a profile is too thin to support a confident recommendation.
