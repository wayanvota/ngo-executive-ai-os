# Proposal Cockpit Routine

## Purpose

Give the executive a clear view of active proposals, reports, renewals, LOIs, and funder deliverables.

## Inputs To Review

1. `memory/fundraising-pipeline.md`
2. relevant donor files
3. relevant project files
4. grant guidelines or funder documents provided by the user
5. prior proposal or report drafts provided by the user

## Output

Produce:

1. `Pipeline Snapshot`: active opportunities and deadlines.
2. `At-Risk Items`: anything blocked, late, weak, or missing evidence.
3. `Drafting Queue`: what should be drafted next.
4. `Review Queue`: what needs executive, program, finance, or board review.
5. `Evidence Gaps`: claims that need source support.
6. `Decision Needed`: the executive decision that would unblock the most value.

Use a table when useful:

| Funder | Item | Amount | Deadline | Stage | Owner | Risk | Next Action |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rules

- Do not invent funder criteria.
- Do not use old grant language without checking whether it still applies.
- Separate restricted and unrestricted opportunities.
- Flag budget, compliance, and impact-claim risk.
