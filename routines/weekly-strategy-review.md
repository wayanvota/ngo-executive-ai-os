# Weekly Strategy Review Routine

## Purpose

Help the executive decide what to focus on, what to stop, and what needs senior judgment.

## Inputs To Review

1. `memory/executive-profile.md`
2. `memory/judgment-rules.md`
3. `memory/fundraising-pipeline.md`
4. `memory/commitments.md`
5. relevant files in `memory/projects/`
6. relevant board or donor files

## Output

Produce:

1. `The Week's Strongest Signal`
2. `What Moved`
3. `What Stalled`
4. `Fundraising Implications`
5. `Board Or Governance Implications`
6. `Strategy Risks`
7. `Decisions Needed`
8. `What To Stop Or Defer`
9. `Next Week's 3 Executive Priorities`

## Rules

- Do not confuse activity with progress.
- Tie strategy to funding, capacity, and evidence.
- Flag vague goals.
- Recommend tradeoffs.
- End with one highest-value next action.
