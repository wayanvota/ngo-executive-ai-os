# Morning Brief Routine

## Purpose

Prepare a concise executive brief for today. Focus on fundraising, strategy, board, and high-value follow-up.

## Inputs To Review

Use available files in this order:

1. `memory/executive-profile.md`
2. `memory/commitments.md`
3. `memory/fundraising-pipeline.md`
4. relevant files in `memory/donors/`
5. relevant files in `memory/board/`
6. relevant files in `memory/projects/`
7. source documents provided by the user

Ask before using connected private accounts.

## Output

Produce:

1. `Today's Focus`: the 1 to 3 decisions or actions that matter most.
2. `Fundraising Attention`: donor, proposal, renewal, or report items needing action.
3. `Strategic Risks`: stalled decisions, weak assumptions, or capacity conflicts.
4. `Meetings To Prepare For`: only meetings that need executive judgment.
5. `Open Commitments`: promises waiting on the executive or someone else.
6. `Suggested Drafts`: emails, notes, or briefings worth drafting, but do not send.
7. `Memory Updates To Consider`: durable facts that should be recorded.

## Rules

- Do not make the brief comprehensive. Make it useful.
- Do not treat every task as equal.
- Flag stale or unsupported memory.
- Recommend the next action clearly.
- Keep donor-facing and board-facing language approval-gated.
